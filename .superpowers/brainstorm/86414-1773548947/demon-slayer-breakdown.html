Demon Slayer Style — Mapped to Art Direction Tiers

Breaking down ufotable's visual language into the knobs we need

Tier 1: Geometry (CPU-side, procedural generation)

What Demon Slayer Does
  • Bold, clean silhouettes — trees read as strong shapes
  • Simplified branching — fewer but more deliberate branches
  • Exaggerated proportions — thicker trunks, wider canopies
  • Organic but not noisy — controlled irregularity
Knobs Needed
  • silhouette_boldness — push proportions toward iconic shapes
  • branch_simplification — reduce count, increase visual weight
  • trunk_taper_exaggeration — more dramatic base-to-tip ratio
  • noise_suppression — smooth out micro-detail in geometry

Tier 2: Shading & Materials (GPU uniform + per-object overrides)

What Demon Slayer Does
  • Soft gradient banding — not hard 2-step cel, but ~3-4 smooth bands
  • Color-shifted shadows — cool purples/blues in shadow, warm in light
  • Exaggerated rim/back lighting — strong edge glow, especially at dusk
  • Saturated, curated palettes — deep greens, rich earth tones
  • Surface detail preserved but "painted" — bark reads as texture, not noise
  • Subsurface scattering on foliage — leaves glow when backlit
Knobs Needed
  • shading_bands — number of discrete light steps (0=smooth PBR, 3-4=anime)
  • band_softness — smoothstep width between bands
  • shadow_color_shift — hue/sat offset for shadowed regions
  • rim_light_strength + rim_light_color
  • palette_saturation — global saturation multiplier
  • palette_hue_shift — rotate entire palette
  • specular_flattening — reduce specular toward matte
  • transmission_boost — backlit leaf glow intensity

Tier 3: Post-Processing (screen-space, global only)

What Demon Slayer Does
  • Variable-weight outlines — thick on silhouettes, thin/absent on interior
  • Atmospheric color grading — scenes have strong overall tint
  • Painterly depth fog — distance objects flatten to background painting
  • Bloom with character — soft, warm glow, not generic HDR bloom
  • No visible paper/halftone texture — clean digital anime look
Knobs Needed
  • outline_strength + outline_thickness
  • outline_depth_sensitivity + outline_normal_sensitivity
  • color_grade_tint + color_grade_strength
  • fog_color_artistic — override physical fog with art-directed color
  • bloom_tint + bloom_softness
  • detail_fade_distance — where objects start flattening

Key Insight

Demon Slayer's look is not traditional cel-shading. It's full 3D simulation → artistic restraint. Your existing PBR pipeline (atmospheric scattering, SSAO, shadow maps, subsurface on bark/foliage) already provides the rich base. The art direction system adds controlled degradation: banding the smooth lighting, shifting the palette, adding drawn edges, and pushing the atmosphere. This is exactly the "high fidelity then desample" approach you described.
